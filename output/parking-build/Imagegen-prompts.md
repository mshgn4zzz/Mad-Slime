# Imagegen

Использован встроенный инструмент Imagegen. Референсы вариаций транспорта сохранены в `../parking-references/Parking-vehicle-variations.png`. Игровая текстура асфальта: `Assets/MadSlime/Resources/LevelSurfaces/Textures/ParkingAsphalt.png`.

## Вариации транспорта

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 3 by 3 reference sheet of NINE different toy-like low-poly parking vehicles for a colorful mobile game. Exactly three columns and three rows, consistent orthographic three-quarter view on pale neutral background, fully separated complete vehicles, large readable silhouettes, flat opaque materials, chunky tires, clean simple bevels, no photoreal textures. Top row three distinct passenger cars: 01 blue compact hatchback with short rounded body, 02 orange sporty coupe with low sloping roof and long hood, 03 green boxy SUV with roof rack and rear spare wheel. Middle row three distinct buses: 04 yellow city bus with flat front and square windows and double doors, 05 turquoise retro minibus with rounded nose and cream roof, 06 red long-distance coach with long dark blue window band and luggage compartments. Bottom row three distinct ARTICULATED SEMI TRUCKS, each must visibly have a separate tractor cab, rear tractor wheels, fifth-wheel connection gap, and long semi-trailer with trailer wheels well behind its center: 07 red tractor + blue closed box semi-trailer, 08 blue tractor + silver cylindrical tanker semi-trailer, 09 orange tractor + green open flatbed semi-trailer carrying three secured stylized wooden crates. All nine vehicles are geometry variations, not just recolors. Keep the friendly low-poly simple game asset style, no logo or brand, no characters, no environment, no extra panels. Label each cell only 01 through 09. No other text, no watermark.

## Асфальт

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless tileable asphalt base albedo texture for a cheerful low-poly mobile game parking lot. Flat orthographic top-down square surface filling the whole image, medium pale cool neutral blue gray base around #858D97. Very quiet hand-painted super cartoon style with sparse broad soft slightly darker and lighter rounded aggregate patches, extremely low contrast, no tiny gravel, no noise, no gritty photoreal detail. The entire surface reads almost as a solid matte gray behind saturated toy cars. Uniform diffuse color only, no baked lighting or shadows. Opposite edges match seamlessly. No road paint, no lines, no arrows, no parking markings, no objects, no cracks, no potholes, no border, no text, no watermark. Road markings will be added as a separate procedural layer in the game.
